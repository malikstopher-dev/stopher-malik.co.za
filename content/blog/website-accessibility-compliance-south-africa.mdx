Accessibility isn't optional — it's legal (POPIA, Equality Act), ethical, and good business. 15%+ of SA users have disabilities.

## WCAG 2.1 AA Checklist

**Perceivable**: Alt text (all images), captions (video), transcripts (audio), colour contrast 4.5:1 (text), 3:1 (UI), resize text 200% no loss, no images of text.

**Operable**: Keyboard navigation (all interactive), focus visible, no keyboard traps, skip links, no timing traps, motion reduction, touch targets 44×44px.

**Understandable**: Language declared, consistent navigation, labels on forms, error identification + suggestion, predictable behaviour.

**Robust**: Valid HTML, ARIA where needed, status messages announced, compatible with assistive tech (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, TalkBack).

## SA-Specific Requirements

POPIA: accessible privacy policy, consent forms. Equality Act: reasonable accommodation. Banking/finance: FSCA accessibility standards. Government: mandatory WCAG 2.1 AA.

## Implementation Priority

- Alt text + colour contrast (highest impact, easiest)
- Keyboard nav + focus
- Form labels + errors
- Heading hierarchy
- ARIA landmarks
- Motion reduction
- Captions/transcripts

## Testing Tools

axe DevTools (browser extension). Lighthouse accessibility. WAVE. NVDA (Windows), VoiceOver (Mac), TalkBack (Android). Keyboard-only navigation test.

## Common SA Failures

Grey text on dark bg (contrast fail). Missing alt text. Form inputs without labels. Carousel without pause. PDF-only documents. No skip link. Video without captions.

## Business Case

Accessible sites: 20% larger addressable market, better SEO, lower legal risk, better usability for everyone (curb cut effect).
