SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) is the practice of improving your website so it appears higher in Google's organic results when people search for what you offer.

## How Google Works (Simplified)

- **Crawl**: Googlebot discovers pages via links and sitemaps.
- **Index**: Pages are analysed and stored in Google's index.
- **Rank**: When someone searches, Google's algorithm orders indexed pages by relevance, authority, and user experience.

## The Three Pillars

**Technical SEO**: Can Google crawl and index your site? Speed, mobile, structure, security, crawlability.
**On-Page SEO**: Does your content match search intent? Keywords, headings, schema, internal links, user experience.
**Off-Page SEO**: Do others vouch for you? Backlinks, reviews, citations, brand mentions.
