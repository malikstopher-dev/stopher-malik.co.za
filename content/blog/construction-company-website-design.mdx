Construction clients buy capability and track record. Your website must prove both before the first meeting.

## Project Portfolio with Filters

Categorise by sector: commercial, residential, industrial, civil. Filter by value, location, completion date. High-quality imagery is non-negotiable.

## Capability Statements

CIDB grading, B-BBEE level, health & safety certifications, plant & equipment list. Downloadable PDF capability statement.

## Team Credibility

Key personnel profiles: qualifications, years experience, notable projects. Put faces to the capability.

## Tender-Ready Content

Standard forms, banking details, tax clearance, letter of good standing. Make it easy for procurement teams to qualify you.

## Contact for Site Visits

Dedicated enquiry form for site inspections. Calendar integration for scheduling.

The Selrahc Architects and construction-adjacent sites we've built all follow this pattern: portfolio first, credibility signals throughout, frictionless enquiry.
