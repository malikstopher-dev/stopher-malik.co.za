Small business websites have one job: convert visitors into enquiries. Every feature must earn its keep.

## Non-Negotiables

**Clear Value Proposition**: Above fold. What, for whom, why better. Five-second test pass. **Contact Accessibility**: Phone (click-to-call), WhatsApp (pre-filled), email, form — all above fold, sticky on mobile. **Trust Signals**: Google reviews (widget), client logos, certifications, physical address, local phone. **Mobile-First**: 70%+ traffic. Thumb-friendly. Fast (under 2.5s LCP). **Analytics**: GA4 + GSC + GBP linked. Conversion events: form, WhatsApp, call, booking.

## Service Pages

One page per core service. H1 = service + location. Benefits > features. Process/timeline. FAQ. CTA. Local schema.

## Content That Converts

About: founder story, team photos, credentials, mission. Case studies: challenge → solution → result (metrics). FAQ: top ten objections answered. Blog: one post/month (service + suburb).

## Technical Basics

SSL. Cloudflare (DNS, cache, WAF, analytics). GA4 + GSC + GBP. Sitemap + robots. Schema: LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ, Breadcrumb. Core Web Vitals monitoring.

## Lead Capture

Form: name, email, phone, service, message. WhatsApp pre-filled. Honeypot spam protection. Web3Forms/email. Auto-reply + WhatsApp notification. CRM integration (optional).

## What to Avoid

Sliders/carousels. PDF menus/brochures. Stock photo clichés. Jargon. Hidden contact. Slow load. Popups on entry. No mobile test.

## Budget Reality

Starter: R1,500–3,000 (landing page). Growth: R4,000–8,000 (five to ten pages, CMS, SEO). Premium: R9,000+ (custom, PWA, content strategy).
