Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is often the first touchpoint for local customers. Optimise it like a landing page.

## Complete Every Field

Business name (exact legal name), category (primary + 4 secondary), address, service areas, hours, phone, website, appointment link, description (750 chars, keyword-rich), attributes (wheelchair accessible, women-led, etc.).

## Photos: Quantity and Quality

100+ photos: exterior, interior, team, projects, products, 360° virtual tour. Update monthly. Geotag. Encourage customer photos.

## Reviews Strategy

Ask every happy client. Provide direct link. Respond to all reviews (keywords in responses help). Flag fake/spam. Never buy reviews.

## Posts and Updates

Weekly posts: offers, events, new services, COVID updates, team spotlights. Use UTM parameters. Track clicks in GBP insights.

## Q&A Management

Seed common questions. Answer promptly. Upvote helpful answers. Monitor for incorrect info.

## Local Citations Consistency

NAP (Name, Address, Phone) identical across: website, GBP, Facebook, LinkedIn, directories (Yellow Pages, Brabys, Hotfrog SA), industry sites.

## Track and Iterate

Monthly: search impressions, map views, direction requests, calls, website clicks, booking clicks. Correlate with lead volume.
