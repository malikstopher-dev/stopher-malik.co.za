Cleaning services sell trust. Your website must communicate reliability, professionalism, and quality before the first interaction.

## Service Clarity

List every service with clear descriptions: residential, commercial, deep cleaning, move-in/out, carpet, windows. Use icons for quick scanning.

## Social Proof Front and Center

Google reviews, before/after photos, client logos, trust badges (insured, vetted staff, eco-friendly products). Place these above the fold.

## Easy Quote Flow

A multi-step quote form: property type → size → frequency → services → contact details. Show progress. Reduce friction.

## Local SEO Integration

Each service area gets a location page: "Cleaning Services in Sandton," "Office Cleaning Johannesburg." Schema markup for LocalBusiness.

## Mobile-First Contact

Click-to-call, WhatsApp button, email form — all thumb-friendly. Emergency/after-hours contact option.

The Cleanisa Solutions site we built uses this exact pattern: service cards, trust-building layout, and a contact conversion flow that delivers qualified leads daily.
