Electrical contractors need websites that communicate safety, compliance, and capability instantly.

## Services Breakdown

Residential: wiring, COC, DB upgrades, lighting, solar. Commercial: reticulation, maintenance contracts, generator install, data/voice. Industrial: motor control, PLC, hazardous areas. List all with clear scope.

## Compliance Front and Center

Wireman's license number. ECSA registration. COC issuance capability. Insurance details. Safety file availability. These are trust prerequisites.

## Project Gallery

Before/after photos. Commercial building reticulation. Solar installations. Generator setups. Caption with scope, value, timeline.

## Emergency Contact Prominence

24/7 emergency number. WhatsApp emergency button. Service area map. Response time commitment.

## Lead Qualification Form

Property type, service needed, urgency, budget range, preferred contact method. Qualifies leads before you call.
