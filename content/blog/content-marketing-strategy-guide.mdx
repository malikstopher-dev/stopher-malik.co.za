Content marketing compounds. Every post you publish is an asset that can rank for years. Here's how to build a strategy that works.

## Keyword-Driven Topics

Start with keyword research. Target informational queries your customers ask: "how to," "what is," "best way to," "cost of." Cluster around pillar topics.

## Pillar-Cluster Architecture

One comprehensive pillar page per core topic. 10–20 cluster posts linking back. Internal linking structure passes authority and keeps visitors exploring.

## Content Calendar

Publish consistently. One high-quality post per week beats five thin posts. Plan quarterly themes aligned with business seasonality.

## Lead Capture in Content

Every post needs a next step: downloadable checklist, calculator, template, newsletter signup. Contextual CTAs convert better than generic ones.

## Distribution

Share on LinkedIn (personal + company), industry WhatsApp groups, email newsletter. Repurpose into carousel posts, short videos.

## Measure What Matters

Track organic traffic, keyword rankings, assisted conversions, newsletter signups. Not just pageviews.

Our own blog follows this model: 50 posts covering web design, SEO, e-commerce, branding — each targeting specific South African search intent.
