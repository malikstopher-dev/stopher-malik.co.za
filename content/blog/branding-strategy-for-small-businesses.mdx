Small businesses in South Africa compete on trust and recognition. A strong brand strategy levels the playing field.

## Define Your Position

Who are you for? What do you do differently? Why should they care? Answer these in one sentence. This is your positioning statement.

## Visual Identity on a Budget

Start with a professional logo and colour palette. Apply consistently across website, social media, invoices, vehicle signage. Consistency builds recognition faster than complexity.

## Voice and Messaging

How do you speak to customers? Professional? Friendly? Direct? Document your tone of voice. Use it in website copy, WhatsApp replies, email signatures.

## Local Relevance

South African customers trust local signals: .co.za domain, local phone number, physical address, POPIA compliance, B-BBEE credentials if applicable.

## Measure Brand Awareness

Track branded search volume, direct traffic, referral mentions. These compound over time and indicate brand health.
