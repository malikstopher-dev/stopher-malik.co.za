Keyword research isn't guessing — it's data-driven market intelligence. Here's how to find terms that convert.

## Start with Seed Topics

List 10–15 core topics: your services, problems you solve, questions prospects ask, competitor keywords, industry terms.

## Use Multiple Tools

Google Keyword Planner (free, SA location), Ubersuggest, AnswerThePublic, Google Autocomplete, Related Searches, Search Console queries. Cross-reference.

## South African Search Nuances

"Apartments to rent Johannesburg" not "flats." "Load shedding solutions" seasonal spike. "Near me" + township names. Afrikaans/English mixes. Mobile-first queries.

## Intent Classification

Informational (blog), Navigational (brand), Commercial (comparison), Transactional (buy/contact). Map each keyword to intent and funnel stage.

## Long-Tail Priority

"Web design Johannesburg" = high volume, high competition. "Affordable web design for plumbers Johannesburg" = lower volume, higher conversion, easier rank. Build topic clusters around long-tails.

## Competitor Gap Analysis

Ahrefs/Semrush content gap: keywords competitors rank for that you don't. Prioritise by difficulty × volume × relevance.

## Local Modifiers

Suburb + service. "Web design Sandton," "SEO Rosebank," "E-commerce developer Pretoria." These convert 3–5× better than broad terms.

## Build Your Keyword Map

Spreadsheet: keyword, volume, difficulty, intent, target page, status (not started/draft/published/ranking). Review monthly.
