In 2027, a business without a website is a business that doesn't exist for 70%+ of potential customers.

## The Numbers

97% of consumers search online before buying locally. 70%+ mobile. 50% visit store within 24hrs of local search. 88% trust online reviews as much as personal recommendations. No website = invisible to all of the above.

## Website ≠ Social Media

Facebook/Instagram: rented land (algorithm changes, reach drops, account risk). Website: owned asset (full control, SEO asset, conversion engine, data ownership). Use social to drive traffic TO website.

## What a Website Does 24/7

Sells your value proposition. Captures leads (forms, WhatsApp, calls). Builds credibility (reviews, case studies, credentials). Answers FAQs (reduces support load). Collects data (analytics, heatmaps, conversion funnels). Enables remarketing (pixel, email capture).

## Cost of Not Having One

Competitor gets the lead. Customer assumes you're not legitimate. No organic visibility. No retargeting. No conversion tracking. No owned audience. Invisible during load shedding (competitor's PWA works offline).

## Minimum Viable Website

Landing page: value prop, services, trust signals, contact (phone/WhatsApp/form), Google Business Profile linked. Cost: R1,500–3,000. Timeline: 1–2 weeks. Our Starter package.

## The Compounding Effect

Year 1: Foundation, first leads. Year 2: SEO traction, organic > paid. Year 3: Authority, referral loops, brand searches. Year 5+: Asset worth 10–50× build cost.

## Common Objections

"My clients don't use the internet." → 97% do. "I get all business from referrals." → Referrals check your site first. "Too expensive." → R1,500 = 2–3 coffees/month. "I have a Facebook page." → You don't own it.

## The Decision

Every day without a professional website, you lose leads to competitors who have one. The cost of building is fixed. The cost of not building compounds.

Start with Starter (R1,500). Upgrade when leads justify it. But start today.
