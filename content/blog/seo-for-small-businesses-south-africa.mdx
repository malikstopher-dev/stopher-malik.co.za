Small businesses in South Africa can win on SEO without enterprise budgets. Focus, consistency, and local relevance beat scale.

## Foundation (Week 1–2)

Google Business Profile (100% complete). Website: fast, mobile, secure, schema. Search Console + GA4. Keyword map: 20–30 target terms (service + suburb).

## Content (Ongoing)

One blog post/week: service + suburb long-tails. "Plumber Sandton emergency," "Accountant Rosebank tax returns." 1500 words. Original photos. Internal links to service pages.

## Technical (Monthly)

Core Web Vitals check. Crawl errors. Index coverage. Sitemap fresh. Robots.txt clean. HTTPS. No mixed content.

## Local Citations (Quarterly)

Consistent NAP on: Google, Facebook, LinkedIn, Yellow Pages, Brabys, Hotfrog, industry directories, chamber of commerce. Audit with BrightLocal/Whitespark.

## Reviews (Continuous)

Ask every client. Google + Facebook + industry site. Respond to all. Keywords in responses. Photos encouraged.

## Link Building (Monthly)

Guest post on 1 SA industry blog. Supplier/partner link. Local sponsorship (school, sports club, charity) with link. Unlinked mention reclamation.

## Tracking (Weekly)

Rankings for target terms (Search Console + rank tracker). Organic traffic + conversions (GA4). GBP insights: calls, direction requests, website clicks.

## Budget Allocation (R5k/month)

R2,000 content. R1,500 technical/tools. R1,000 citations/link building. R500 review management.

## Common SA Mistakes

Ignoring WhatsApp as conversion channel. No Google Business Profile. Targeting national keywords only. Buying cheap links. Expecting 2-week results.

SEO is a 6–12 month investment. Month 3: first rankings. Month 6: traffic growth. Month 12: compounding leads.
