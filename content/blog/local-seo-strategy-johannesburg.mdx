Johannesburg local search is neighbourhood-specific. "Web designer Sandton" ≠ "Web designer Soweto." Here's how to own your area.

## Suburb-Level Landing Pages

One page per service area: "Web Design Sandton," "Web Design Rosebank," "Web Design Fourways." Unique content: local landmarks, client case studies, area-specific keywords, embedded map.

## Google Business Profile Per Location

If you serve multiple suburbs from one office, use service areas. If you have physical locations, create separate GBP listings. Optimise each.

## Local Schema Markup

LocalBusiness with geo-coordinates, service areas, opening hours, price range, payment accepted, area served (City, Suburb). Test with Rich Results Test.

## Citations with Suburb Precision

Directory listings: "123 Main St, Sandton, Johannesburg, 2196" not just "Johannesburg." Consistent across all platforms.

## Review Geography

Encourage reviews mentioning suburb: "Great web designer in Sandton!" Google extracts location entities from review text.

## Local Content

Blog posts: "Top 10 Restaurants in Sandton," "Load Shedding Schedule for Fourways Businesses," "Best Coworking Spaces in Rosebank." Natural internal links to service pages.

## Competitor Map Pack Analysis

Search "your service + suburb." Analyse top 3: GBP completeness, review count/velocity, photo quantity, website authority, citation consistency. Beat them systematically.

## Hyper-Local Keywords

"Web designer near Sandton City," "SEO agency Rosebank Mall," "E-commerce developer near Gautrain." These convert at 2–3× broad terms.
