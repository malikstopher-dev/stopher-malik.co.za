Speed is a ranking factor, conversion factor, and trust factor. Here's the playbook for SA conditions.

## LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) under 2.5s

• **Critical CSS**: inline above-fold styles.
• **Preload hero image**: `<link rel="preload" as="image">`.
• **Optimise hero**: WebP/AVIF, correct dimensions, `fetchpriority="high"`.
• **Server**: Cloudflare Pages (edge), TTFB under 600ms.
• **Font**: `font-display: swap`, preload variable fonts, subset Latin.

## INP (Interaction to Next Paint) under 200ms

• **Main thread**: minimise JS. Code splitting (route-level). `defer` non-critical scripts. Web Workers for heavy calc. Debounce/throttle handlers. Avoid layout thrashing.

## CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift) under 0.1

• **Dimensions**: width/height on all images, iframes, ads.
• **Font fallback**: `size-adjust`, `ascent-override`, `descent-override`, `line-gap-override`.
• **Dynamic content**: reserve space (skeleton loaders).
• **Animations**: `transform`/`opacity` only, no layout-triggering.

## Image Optimisation

• **Format**: WebP (all), AVIF (progressive).
• **Responsive**: `srcset` + `sizes`, `picture` for art direction.
• **Lazy load**: `loading="lazy"` below fold.
• **CDN**: Cloudflare Images / Cloudinary auto-format.

## Caching Strategy

• **Static**: immutable, `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable`.
• **HTML**: `stale-while-revalidate=86400`.
• **API**: `stale-while-revalidate=60`.
• **Service Worker**: cache-first static, network-first HTML, background sync forms.

## SA-Specific

• **Data costs**: aggressive compression, subset fonts, minimal JS.
• **Load shedding**: PWA offline, service worker cache-first.
• **Mobile networks**: 3G throttle testing mandatory.
• **Edge**: Cloudflare Cape Town/Johannesburg PoPs.

## Tools & Budgets

Lighthouse CI (every PR). WebPageTest (3G Cape Town). Chrome DevTools Performance. Bundle analyzer (`@next/bundle-analyzer`). Budget: under 100KB CSS, under 200KB JS, under 500KB total, under 50 requests.

Every site we build ships with: critical CSS, preload hero, WebP images, Cloudflare edge, service worker, Lighthouse CI gate.
