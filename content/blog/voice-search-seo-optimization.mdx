Voice search is growing: "Hey Google, web designer near me." Here's how to capture it.

## Voice Query Characteristics

Longer (seven-plus words), conversational, question-based ("how do I," "where can I," "what's the best"), local intent ("near me," "in Sandton"), immediate need.

## Content Optimisation

FAQ schema on every service page. Questions as H2/H3: "How much does web design cost in Johannesburg?" Answer in 40 to 60 words (featured snippet length). Conversational tone.

## Local Business Schema

Complete LocalBusiness: name, address, phone, hours, geo, priceRange, paymentAccepted, areaServed (suburbs), serviceType. GBP consistency.

## Featured Snippet Targeting

List snippets: numbered steps. Paragraph snippets: direct answer + context. Table snippets: pricing comparison. Video snippets: how-to.

## Technical

Page speed (voice = mobile). HTTPS. Structured data valid. No crawl errors. Mobile-first indexing.

## SA Voice Landscape

Google Assistant (English, Afrikaans, isiZulu, isiXhosa). Alexa (limited SA). Siri (English). "Near me" + township/suburb names. Load shedding queries spike ("generator installer near me").

## Measurement

Search Console: query impressions for question keywords. GA4: voice traffic segment (regex: `what|how|where|when|why|who|best|near`). GBP: "direction requests," "calls" from voice-initiated searches.

## Quick Wins

Add FAQ section to top ten pages. Claim GBP + 100 photos. Target five "near me" keywords per suburb. Page speed under 2.5s LCP.

Voice won't replace typed search — but capturing it now builds authority for the conversational future.
