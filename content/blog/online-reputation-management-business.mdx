Your reputation precedes every sales conversation. Manage it proactively.

## Monitor Everything

Google Alerts (brand, founders, key products), Mention, Awario, ReviewTrackers. Real-time notifications for new reviews, mentions, social posts.

## Review Platform Coverage

Google, Facebook, HelloPeter, Trustpilot, industry-specific (TripAdvisor, Zomato, Checkatrade). Claim all profiles. Enable notifications.

## Response Protocol

Respond within 24 hours. Positive: thank + personal detail. Negative: acknowledge, apologise, offer resolution offline, follow up publicly when resolved. Never argue.

## Review Generation System

Ask at peak satisfaction: post-project, post-support, post-purchase. Provide direct links. SMS/WhatsApp/email templates. Automate with CRM.

## Crisis Preparation

Escalation matrix. Pre-approved holding statements. Spokesperson designated. Legal review for serious issues. Social media pause protocol.

## Content Suppression

Publish positive content: case studies, client wins, community involvement, thought leadership. Optimise for branded terms. Push negative results to page 2+.

## Employee Advocacy

Guidelines for personal social media. Encourage sharing company content. LinkedIn employee notifications. Glassdoor management.

## Legal Recourse

Defamation: letter of demand → court. POPIA violations: Information Regulator. Fake reviews: platform reporting + legal if needed.

Reputation compounds. A 4.7★ average with 50+ reviews converts 3–5× better than 4.0★ with 5 reviews.
