Traffic without conversion is vanity. CRO turns existing visitors into enquiries, sales, bookings.

## Above-the-Fold Clarity

Value proposition, primary CTA, trust signal — all visible without scrolling. Test headline variations. The "digital experiences with depth" headline on our homepage went through 12 iterations.

## Form Optimisation

Fewer fields = higher completion. Only ask for what you need to qualify. Multi-step forms for complex enquiries. Inline validation. Clear error messages.

## Trust Signals

Google reviews, client logos, case studies, certifications, security badges, money-back guarantees. Place near CTAs and forms.

## WhatsApp Integration

South Africa communicates on WhatsApp. Click-to-chat button, pre-filled message, business profile. Track as a conversion event.

## Speed = Conversions

Every 100ms delay drops conversions ~7%. Optimise LCP, reduce CLS, minimise main-thread blocking. Our sites target sub-2.5s LCP on 3G.

## A/B Testing Culture

Test one element at a time. Statistical significance before deciding. Document learnings. Build a testing roadmap.

The JMOTO Electrical site saw a 34% enquiry increase after we simplified the contact form and added a sticky WhatsApp button.
