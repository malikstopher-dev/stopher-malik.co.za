Colour isn't decoration — it's communication. The right palette guides attention, builds trust, and drives action.

## South African Context

Blue = trust, banking, corporate. Green = growth, sustainability, finance. Orange/Red = urgency, food, retail. Purple = premium, creative, tech. Choose based on your industry and audience.

## Primary Action Colour

One dominant colour for CTAs. High contrast against background. Consistent across all buttons, links, form submissions. Our accent #378ADD works because it stands out on dark backgrounds without being aggressive.

## Emotional Hierarchy

Background: calm, recedes. Content: readable, accessible. Accent: draws eye, signals action. Never compete for attention.

## Accessibility First

WCAG AA contrast ratios: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text and UI components. Test every combination. Don't sacrifice usability for aesthetics.

## Cultural Sensitivity

Red means different things in different South African communities. Research your audience. When in doubt, test.

The Boma Cafe site uses a warm coral accent (#FF6B4A) that stimulates appetite and feels premium — perfect for a restaurant brand.
