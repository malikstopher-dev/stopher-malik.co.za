Not either/or — it's sequence and budget allocation. Here's the framework.

## SEO: The Asset You Build

Pros: Compounding returns, trust signal, lower CAC long-term, brand authority, covers full funnel. Cons: 6–12 months to meaningful traffic, algorithm risk, requires expertise, hard to scale quickly.

## Paid Ads: The Tap You Turn On

Pros: Immediate traffic, precise targeting, measurable ROI, scalable, tests messaging fast. Cons: Stops when spend stops, rising CPCs, ad fatigue, trust lower, no asset value.

## The Hybrid Strategy (Recommended)

**Month 1–3**: 80% paid / 20% SEO. Build foundation, test messaging, get immediate leads, fund SEO.
**Month 4–6**: 60% paid / 40% SEO. SEO traction starts. Shift budget as CAC improves.
**Month 7–12**: 40% paid / 60% SEO. SEO compounds. Paid for: brand defence, new service tests, seasonal spikes.
**Year 2+**: 20% paid / 80% SEO. SEO = primary channel. Paid = strategic accelerator.

## Channel-Specific Nuances

**Google Ads**: High intent, high CPC. Best for: emergency services, high-ticket B2B, e-commerce.
**Facebook/Instagram Ads**: Lower intent, lower CPC. Best for: visual products, local services, brand awareness.
**LinkedIn Ads**: B2B only. Expensive. Best for: high-ticket professional services.

## SA Market Reality

CPCs rising 15–20% YoY. Load shedding = mobile traffic spikes. WhatsApp conversion tracking essential. POPIA compliant lead forms.

## Decision Framework

| If... | Start With |
|-------|------------|
| Need leads this week | Paid |
| Budget < R10k/month | SEO first |
| High-ticket, long sales cycle | SEO + LinkedIn |
| Visual product, impulse buy | Paid social |
| Local service business | SEO (GBP + local) |
| New market/test offer | Paid |
