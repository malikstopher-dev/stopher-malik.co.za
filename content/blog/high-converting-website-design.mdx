Conversion isn't luck — it's applied psychology. Every element should reduce friction or increase motivation.

## Value Proposition Clarity

Headline + subheadline + hero image must answer: What? For whom? Why better? In 5 seconds. Our "Digital experiences with depth" tells you we're premium, technical, and outcome-focused.

## Social Proof Architecture

Logos above fold. Testimonials with photos/names/roles. Case studies with metrics. Trust badges. Reviews with stars. Place at every decision point.

## Scarcity and Urgency (Ethical)

"Only 3 slots this month." "Price increases 1st Jan." "Limited to 10 clients." Real constraints, not fake countdown timers.

## Risk Reversal

Money-back guarantee. Free audit. No-contract trial. Pilot project. Remove the fear of wrong decision.

## Cognitive Load Reduction

One primary action per screen. Progressive disclosure. Familiar patterns. Clear visual hierarchy. White space is not wasted space.

## Mobile-First Persuasion

Thumb zones for CTAs. Sticky header with contact. Click-to-call/WhatsApp. Simplified forms. Fast load = trust.

The Salem Home Innovation site applies these principles: clear value prop, sticky WhatsApp, sticky bottom nav, trust badges, simple quote form — generating enquiries from day one.
