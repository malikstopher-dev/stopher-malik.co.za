The answer depends on your content depth and conversion strategy.

## Single-Page (Landing Page)

Best for: one offer, one audience, one action. Campaign landing pages, product launches, event registration, freelancer portfolio, simple service.

Pros: Focused narrative, controlled flow, fast to build, easy A/B testing, high conversion for single action. Cons: Limited SEO (one title, one H1), thin content for competitive terms, analytics blind spots, hard to scale.

## Multi-Page (Traditional Site)

Best for: multiple services, multiple audiences, SEO strategy, credibility building, long-term asset.

Pros: Targeted SEO per page, deep content, trust signals, scalable, analytics granularity, internal linking. Cons: More complex, higher build cost, maintenance, potential navigation friction.

## Hybrid: Single-Page Feel, Multi-Page Structure

Home page = long-form sales page (single-page flow). Service pages = SEO targets. Blog = content marketing. About/Contact = trust. This is what we build for 80% of clients.

## Decision Matrix

| Scenario | Recommendation |
|--------|----------------|
| One service, one location, lead gen | Single-page |
| Multiple services, SEO growth | Multi-page |
| Campaign-specific (3 months) | Single-page |
| Brand building, 2+ years | Multi-page |
| E-commerce | Multi-page |
| Personal brand/portfolio | Single-page |

## SEO Reality

Single-page ranks for 1–3 keywords max. Multi-page scales to 50–200+. If organic growth matters, multi-page wins.

## Our Approach

Starter Package (R1,500–3,000): Single-page optimised for conversion. Business Growth (R4,000–8,000): Multi-page with SEO foundation. Premium (R9,000+): Multi-page + content strategy + PWA.
