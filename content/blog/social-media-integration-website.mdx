Social media on your website should drive traffic both ways — not just be decorative icons.

## Social Proof Embedding

Instagram feed (UGC, tagged posts). Facebook reviews widget. LinkedIn company updates. Twitter/X latest tweets. Curate, don't auto-publish everything.

## Share & Follow Strategy

Share buttons: blog posts, portfolio items, case studies. Floating share bar on scroll. WhatsApp share (huge in SA). Follow buttons: header, footer, thank-you pages, email signatures.

## Social Login / Auth

Google, Facebook, LinkedIn, Apple. Reduces form friction. Capture email for marketing. POPIA: clear consent, data minimisation.

## Open Graph / Twitter Cards

Every page: og:title, og:description, og:image (1200×630), og:url, twitter:card (summary_large_image). Test with Facebook Sharing Debugger, Twitter Card Validator.

## UGC Gallery

Hashtag campaign (#SMKWebDesign). Moderated feed on website. Incentivise: feature on homepage, discount, giveaway. Rights management (T&Cs for UGC use).

## WhatsApp Integration

Click-to-chat on every page. Pre-filled messages per page context. Business profile with catalogue. Quick replies. Label conversations (enquiry, support, existing client).

## Analytics

UTM parameters on all social links. GA4: social source/medium, conversion by network. Track: shares, follows, WhatsApp clicks, referral traffic quality.

## Common Mistakes

Dead social links (404). Auto-posting blog to LinkedIn without native format. 20 icon links in footer. No WhatsApp. Feeds showing 2020 content.

Our sites include: OG/Twitter cards on every page, WhatsApp CTA sticky, curated Instagram feed, share buttons on content, follow in header/footer.
