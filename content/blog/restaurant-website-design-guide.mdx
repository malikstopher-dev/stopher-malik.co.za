Restaurant websites have one job: get butts in seats. Every element serves that goal.

## Menu as Conversion Tool

Digital menu (not PDF). Categories, descriptions, prices, dietary tags (vegan, gluten-free, halal). High-quality photos for signature dishes. Mobile-first, fast, no zoom needed.

## Booking Integration

ResDiary, NowBookIt, SevenRooms, OpenTable, custom. Embed or prominent CTA. Real-time availability. Party size, date, time, occasion, special requests. Confirmation SMS/WhatsApp.

## Location & Hours

Prominent: address, map, parking, public transport. Hours (split for kitchen/bar). Holiday hours. Google Business Profile sync.

## Gallery & Atmosphere

Interior, exterior, dishes, drinks, events, team. Professional photography. Video walkthrough. Instagram feed integration.

## Online Ordering (If Applicable)

Click & collect, delivery (UberEats, Mr D, OrderIn, own drivers). Menu sync. Kitchen ticket printing. POS integration.

## Events & Private Dining

Calendar, capacity, packages, enquiry form, deposit payment. SEO for "private dining Johannesburg," "year-end function venue Sandton."

## Reviews & Social Proof

Google/TripAdvisor/Zomato widgets. UGC gallery (tagged Instagram posts). Press mentions. Awards.

## Speed & Mobile

Hungry users on 3G. Under 2s LCP. Thumb-friendly booking CTA. Sticky header with phone/booking. No auto-play video/audio.

The Boma Cafe site we built: bilingual menu, gallery, booking flow, events — reservations up 40% in month one.
