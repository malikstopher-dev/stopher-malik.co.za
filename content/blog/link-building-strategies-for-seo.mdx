Links remain a top-3 ranking factor. But South African link building requires local relevance and relationship-first approach.

## Foundational Links (Do These First)

Google Business Profile, social profiles, industry directories (Yellow Pages, Brabys, Hotfrog, SaBusinessIndex), chamber of commerce, B-BBEE certificate listings, supplier/partner pages, client testimonials on their sites.

## Content-Driven Links

Original research/data (survey 100+ SA businesses), free tools/calculators, ultimate guides, templates/checklists, infographics with embed code. Outreach: "I created this resource for [audience], thought your readers would find it valuable."

## Local PR and News

Media releases for: business milestones, community initiatives, expert commentary on industry trends, award wins, new service launches. Target: BusinessTech, ITWeb, MyBroadband, local newspapers, industry blogs.

## Guest Posting (Selective)

High-authority SA sites only: BizCommunity, Entrepreneur SA, SME South Africa, industry-specific publications. One quality post > ten low-quality. Always disclose relationship.

## Broken Link Building

Find broken links on relevant SA sites. Create better replacement content. Outreach: "I noticed a broken link on [page], I've created an updated resource here."

## Unlinked Brand Mentions

Monitor brand mentions (Google Alerts, Mention, Awario). Request link addition where appropriate.

## Avoid

Link farms, PBNs, paid links (unless nofollow/sponsored), reciprocal link schemes, comment spam, directory submission services.
