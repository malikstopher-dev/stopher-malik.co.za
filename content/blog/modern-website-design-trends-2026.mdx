Trends serve business goals, not the other way around. Here's what's working in SA right now.

## In: Dark Mode by Default

Dark backgrounds, vibrant accents, reduced eye strain, OLED battery savings. Our entire design system is dark-first with cyan accent (#378ADD).

## In: Micro-Interactions with Purpose

Button press states, form validation feedback, scroll progress, loading skeletons, page transitions. Every interaction communicates state.

## In: Variable Fonts

Single font file, infinite weights. Inter + Bai Jamjuree variable. Smaller payload, finer typographic control.

## In: Semantic HTML + CSS Grid

Clean markup, accessible by default, maintainable. Grid for layout, Flexbox for components. No framework bloat.

## In: Privacy-First Analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics, Plausible, Umami. No cookies, GDPR/POPIA compliant, lightweight.

## Out: Hero Sliders/Carousels

Low engagement, slow, accessibility issues. Static hero with clear value prop converts better.

## Out: Parallax Overload

Performance cost, motion sickness, mobile broken. Subtle depth (our cinematic hero) > aggressive parallax.

## Out: Popup Overload

Cookie banners, newsletter popups, chat widgets, exit intent — all at once. One at a time, timed, dismissible.

## Out: Stock Photo Clichés

Handshakes, diverse team pointing at laptop, generic cityscapes. Custom photography or quality illustrations.

## Out: Hamburger Menu on Desktop

Hidden navigation = lower discovery. Visible nav for 5–7 items. Prioritise ruthlessly.

The trends that stay are the ones that improve conversion, accessibility, or performance. Everything else is noise.
