The most common question. Here's transparent 2026 pricing for the South African market.

## Single-Page / Landing Page

R1,500–R3,000. One scrollable page. Contact form. Basic SEO. 1–2 weeks. Good for: freelancers, events, simple services.

## Small Business (5–10 Pages)

R4,000–R8,000. Home, About, Services, Portfolio, Contact, Blog. CMS. Google Business setup. Schema. 3–4 weeks. Our most popular package.

## E-Commerce Store

R6,000–R20,000+. Product catalogue, cart, checkout, payments (PayFast/Yoco/PayPal), courier integration, inventory, abandoned cart. 4–8 weeks.

## Custom Web Application

R15,000–R100,000+. Client portals, booking systems, calculators, dashboards, API integrations. Bespoke scope.

## Ongoing Costs (Annual)

Hosting: R500–R3,000. Domain: R100–R300. SSL: Free (Let's Encrypt) – R2,000 (EV). Maintenance retainer: R1,500–R5,000/month. Content updates: R500–R2,000/page.

## What Drives Cost Up

Custom design system. Complex animations. Third-party integrations. Multilingual. High-volume e-commerce. Rush timeline.
