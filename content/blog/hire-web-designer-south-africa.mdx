Hiring a web designer is a business decision, not a creative one. Here's how to evaluate properly.

## Portfolio Quality Over Quantity

10 exceptional projects > 50 mediocre ones. Look for: similar industry, similar complexity, measurable results (traffic, leads, sales), live sites you can test.

## Technical Competence

CMS choice and why. Hosting and performance. SEO foundation. Security practices. Maintenance plan. Ask: "How do you handle Core Web Vitals?"

## Process Transparency

Discovery → strategy → design → development → testing → launch → support. Fixed timeline. Clear deliverables at each stage. No black boxes.

## Communication Style

Responsive? Proactive? Explains technical concepts simply? Weekly updates? Single point of contact? You're hiring a partner, not a vendor.

## Pricing Clarity

Fixed price for defined scope. Change request process. No hidden costs (hosting, SSL, plugins, stock photos, revisions). Our packages: Starter R1,500–3,000, Growth R4,000–8,000, Premium R9,000+.

## Post-Launch Support

Training included? Analytics handover? 30-day bug fix warranty? Retainer options? SLA for critical issues?

Red flags: "unlimited revisions," no contract, no portfolio, cheapest quote, promises #1 Google ranking.
