The choice between custom and template isn't binary — it's about trade-offs aligned with your stage and goals.

## Template: Fast, Cheap, Limited

R500–R5,000. Days to launch. Limited customisation. Shared codebase = slower, potential conflicts. Good for: testing an idea, very tight budget, temporary presence.

## Custom: Strategic Asset

R5,000–R50,000+. Weeks to launch. Built for your exact needs. Clean code, optimal performance, scalable. Good for: established businesses, competitive markets, long-term growth.

## The Hidden Costs of Templates

Plugin bloat, security vulnerabilities, design constraints, SEO limitations, rebranding difficulties. You often rebuild within 12–18 months.

## When to Invest in Custom

You're spending on paid ads (wasted on poor landing pages). Competitors outrank you. You need specific functionality (booking, client portal, calculators). Brand differentiation matters.

## Hybrid Approach

Custom design system + component library + CMS. Best of both: strategic foundation, content flexibility. This is what our Business Growth package (R4,000–8,000) delivers.
