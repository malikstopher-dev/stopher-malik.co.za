South African e-commerce has unique requirements: payment gateways, logistics, trust, mobile-first buyers.

## Payment Gateway Stack

PayFast (cards, EFT, capitec), Yoco (cards, tap-to-pay), PayPal (international), Ozow (instant EFT), SnapScan/Zapper (QR). Offer 3+ options. Test each flow.

## Logistics Integration

CourierGuy, Fastway, Aramex, PostNet, Pudo. Real-time rates at checkout. Free shipping threshold (R750–R1,000 typical). Collection points for township/rural reach.

## Trust Signals for SA Shoppers

SSL padlock visible. Company registration number. Physical address. Returns policy (7–14 days). WhatsApp support. Reviews from local buyers.

## Product Data for SEO

Structured data: Product, Offer, AggregateRating, Review. Clean URLs. Faceted navigation with canonical tags. XML sitemap auto-generated.

## Mobile Checkout Optimisation

Thumb-friendly inputs. Address autocomplete. Saved payment methods. Guest checkout. Progress indicator. Load time ~3s on 3G.

## Abandoned Cart Recovery

Email + WhatsApp sequence: ~1hr, 24hr, 72hr. Discount on final attempt. Track recovery revenue.
