Your architecture website is your most important portfolio piece. It needs to showcase your work beautifully while converting visitors into enquiries.

## Portfolio-First Design

Lead with your best projects. Use large, high-quality images with minimal chrome. Let the work speak.

## Project Case Studies

Each project should tell a story: brief, challenge, solution, outcome. Include client testimonials where possible.

## Technical Credibility

Show your technical capabilities: BIM integration, sustainability credentials, planning experience. This builds trust with developer clients.

## Contact Flow

Make it effortless to start a conversation. A simple project enquiry form with file upload for briefs.

## Mobile Performance

Architecture clients browse on tablets and phones during site visits. Your portfolio must load fast and look pristine on every device.

The Selrahc Architects site we built demonstrates these principles: bilingual support, project filtering, and an enquiry system that qualifies leads before the first call.
