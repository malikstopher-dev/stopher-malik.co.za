"When will I rank #1?" The honest answer: it depends, and it takes time. Here's why.

## Google's Trust Building

New site = no history. Google tests: crawl, index, show for low-competition queries, measure user signals (CTR, dwell, bounce), gradually expand. This is the "sandbox" — real, not a penalty.

## Competition Compounds

Your competitors aren't static. They're publishing, linking, optimising. To overtake, you must build more authority faster. Authority = quality content × quality links × time.

## Content Velocity Matters

One post/month = slow authority build. One post/week = 4× faster topical coverage. Quality > quantity, but consistency enables compounding.

## Link Acquisition is Slow

White-hat links: outreach, relationships, content assets. 1–4 quality links/month is realistic. 50+ referring domains = competitive for mid-difficulty terms.

## Algorithm Updates

Core updates (2–3×/year) reshuffle. Sites with thin content, poor UX, spammy links drop. Sites with E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) rise. Consistency buffers volatility.

## The Compounding Curve

Month 1–3: Technical foundation, GBP, keyword map, first content. Month 4–6: First page 2–3 rankings, branded traffic growth. Month 7–12: Page 1 for long-tails, organic leads start. Month 13–24: Head terms, compounding traffic, referral links. Year 3+: Authority asset, dominates niche.

## What Accelerates

Technical perfection (Core Web Vitals, schema, crawl health). High-quality content (original data, expert authorship). Strategic links (industry authority, local relevance). Consistency (no gaps >2 weeks).

## What Delays

Technical debt. Thin/duplicate content. Spammy links. Inconsistent publishing. Ignoring local SEO. Expecting 2-week results.

## Our Timeline Commitment

Month 3: Technical audit clean, GBP optimised, keyword map live, 5+ content pieces. Month 6: 10+ page 1 rankings (long-tail), measurable organic traffic. Month 12: 30+ page 1 rankings, organic leads > paid.

SEO is a 12-month minimum commitment. The businesses that understand this win.
