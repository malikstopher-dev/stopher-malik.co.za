South African SMEs need a digital strategy that works with local constraints: budget, bandwidth, skills, market nuances.

## Foundation First

Website (conversion-optimised), Google Business Profile, basic analytics, Search Console. These are non-negotiable. Everything else builds on them.

## Channel Prioritisation

- **Organic Search** — highest ROI long-term. Invest here first.
- **WhatsApp Business** — where SA customers are. Catalogue, quick replies, labels.
- **Google Ads** — only when SEO foundation exists and you have conversion tracking.
- **Social Media** — LinkedIn for B2B, Instagram/Facebook for B2C. Organic > paid initially.
- **Email/WhatsApp Newsletter** — own your audience. Segment by interest.

## Budget Allocation (R10k/month example)

R4,000 SEO/content. R3,000 Google Ads (branded + high-intent). R2,000 WhatsApp/social tools. R1,000 analytics/tools.

## Measurement Framework

Weekly: leads, source, cost per lead. Monthly: SEO rankings, traffic quality, channel ROI. Quarterly: strategy review, budget reallocation.

## Common SA Pitfalls

Buying likes/followers. Boosting posts without pixel. No conversion tracking. Ignoring WhatsApp. Treating digital as "marketing's job" not business strategy.
